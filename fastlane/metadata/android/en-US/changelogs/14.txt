Data-integrity and installer safety release.

- APK + OBB installs are now rollback-safe; a failed install no longer replaces expansion files.
- Debloat definitions are signed, verified against a pinned key, and activated atomically.
- Local metadata, snapshots, and automation state now fail closed instead of losing data.
- App updates are audited for newly exported components and weakened permission guards.
- Archive extraction is contained against symlink redirection.
